What if you could mine Bitcoin in 2008?

the miner

Abstract

If you knew what Bitcoin would become — a trillion-dollar network born from just a few lines of code — would you have mined it back in 2008? Most people would say yes. Yet when opportunity appears, it rarely looks like what the future will prove it to be. This paper presents a decentralized system inspired by that paradox — a token protocol that recreates the early conditions of Bitcoin: open, fair, and belief-driven. It challenges the reader not to imagine the profits of hindsight, but to act with the conviction that early miners once had — when there was no guarantee, no market, and no recognition, only code and trust.
